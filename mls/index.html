How to read this report

Price comparison confidence grades quote coverage, spread and freshness. Optional squad, schedule and xG confidence is shown separately. It is not the chance that Favorite-NO wins.

Signals: 🟢 supports considering NO, 🟡 is neutral/watch, 🔴 opposes NO, and ⚪ means unavailable. They are screening directions, not predictions.

Recent absence unconfirmed means a previously regular player was missing from the latest observed matchday squad. It is review context only, carries zero model weight, and is not a claim that the player is injured or will miss the upcoming fixture.

Classification categories

  • Insufficient price data: a required quote is missing or over 60 minutes old.
  • No current Favorite-NO case: consensus is at least 2 pp less favorable than PM and contextual support is insufficient.
  • Market aligned: the price gap is strictly between −2 and +2 pp.
  • Moderate price discrepancy: consensus favors NO by 2–4 pp.
  • Material price discrepancy: consensus favors NO by at least 4 pp.
  • Schedule, approximate travel and squad flags are optional screening context. Missing context does not block the odds comparison; no flag is not an all-clear. These indicators do not establish an overlooked market edge.
  • Context-supported price discrepancy: a 2+ pp gap is reinforced by sufficient rest, rotation, availability, or xG pressure.
  • Context watch only: contextual pressure is notable even though the price gap is below 2 pp.

Price signal

Price signal is the de-vigged sportsbook consensus minus PM NO ask:

  • Below 2 pp: alignment or likely noise.
  • 2–4 pp: moderate discrepancy.
  • 4+ pp: material discrepancy.

Review priority is an experimental sorting aid, not expected value.

Polymarket prices select the favorite; sportsbook consensus is the principal external strength baseline. League and xG standings are supporting context. xG receives ×0.12 at 0–4 matches, ×0.25 at 5–9, ×0.50 at 10–14, ×0.75 at 15–19, and ×1.00 at 20+. Limited xG samples reduce context confidence without invalidating the price comparison. There is no independent Elo, power-rating, or player-value model.

Probabilities are shown as percentages. Median and lowest raw bookmaker probabilities are context only, not wagering recommendations. Quote ages are measured at publication. Prices do not guarantee a fill; missing values mean unavailable data, never zero.
