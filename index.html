How to read this report

Overall confidence is the weakest material grade across price, squad, schedule, and xG inputs. It measures interpretability, not the chance that Favorite-NO wins.

Signals: 🟢 supports considering NO, 🟡 is neutral/watch, 🔴 opposes NO, and ⚪ means unavailable. They are screening directions, not predictions.

Price signal is de-vigged sportsbook consensus minus PM NO ask. Under 2 pp is alignment/noise, 2–4 pp a moderate discrepancy, and 4+ pp material. Review priority is an experimental sorting aid, not expected value.

Polymarket prices select the favorite; de-vigged sportsbook consensus is the principal external strength baseline. League and xG standings are supporting context. xG receives ×0.12 at 0–4 matches, ×0.25 at 5–9, ×0.50 at 10–14, ×0.75 at 15–19, and ×1.00 at 20+. There is no independent Elo, power-rating, or player-value model.

Probabilities are shown as percentages. Median and lowest raw bookmaker probabilities are context only, not wagering recommendations. Quote ages are measured at publication. Prices do not guarantee a fill; missing values mean unavailable data, never zero.
